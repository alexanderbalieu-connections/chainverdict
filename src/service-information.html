Privacy and service information

For ChainVerdict, Pulse and TradeRails. Contact contact@chainverdict.xyz about data, accessibility, payment or service issues.

What leaves your device

  • Inspector and the downloaded evidence checker. Inspector processes pasted captures in your browser. The downloaded checker evaluates inputs locally and uses network access when you ask it to fetch public evidence.
  • Live checks and APIs. The service receives the URL or inputs you submit to perform the check. Only use public endpoint URLs for the live checker. URLs can appear in browser history, infrastructure logs and downstream requests. Keep keys, private URLs and sensitive identifiers out of examples and support emails.
  • Payments. Paid API requests use x402 with USDC on Base. The payment facilitator processes payment authorization, and settled transfers are visible on the public blockchain. We use service and settlement records to troubleshoot requests and reconcile payments.
  • Email. We use information you send to investigate your issue and respond. For a payment problem, start with the endpoint and approximate UTC time.

Email us to ask about personal data connected to your use of the service or to exercise applicable privacy rights.

What results mean

Results are informational and may have limited coverage or become stale. Check the source and date before relying on a result. The service does not provide financial, legal or compliance advice. A signature establishes which key signed a response. A timestamp establishes that a commitment existed by a given time. Neither establishes that the underlying observation was correct.
